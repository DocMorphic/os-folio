# Alpine panorama

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Original PNG preserved at 2172 × 724, including alpha; no resizing or recompression. Used as distant cylindrical scenery, not a photograph of a named real location.

## Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: transparent PNG panoramic terrain backdrop for a cylindrical 3D alpine lake world, with sky rendered separately behind it.
Primary request: Generate ONE high-resolution ultrawide 3:1 landscape image, ideally 3072x1024, of a PHOTOREALISTIC alpine mountain and forest shoreline CUTOUT on genuine transparent alpha.
Subject: Dense, realistically detailed mixed evergreen forest forms a continuous roughly level distant lakeshore along the bottom edge. Behind it rise dramatically tall rugged gray alpine peaks, craggy limestone and granite strata, narrow snow gullies, and receding blue-gray massifs. Irregular natural mountain silhouette, with a low valley near the center. Rich real photographic rock and tree texture throughout.
Composition/framing: Panoramic eye-level view from across a lake, but show ONLY the terrain. Terrain fills the full bottom edge; forest-covered foothills at both left and right edges are similar in height, lighting, and texture so the strip can wrap horizontally with minimal seam. Peaks must fit fully inside the image with transparent padding above them. Broad landscape, not a narrow isolated island.
Lighting/mood: Neutral soft daytime sunlight, natural restrained greens and stone grays, subtle atmospheric depth while preserving realistic texture.
Critical transparency: All space above the exact mountain-and-treetop silhouette must be genuinely transparent alpha, including the central valley opening. Clean intricate natural cutout edges, no sky color fringe. This must composite over separately rendered day or night sky. No sky pixels, clouds, solid background, white background, or fake checkerboard.
Avoid: NO water, NO lake surface, NO reflection, NO buildings, NO people, NO text, NO watermark, NO cartoon, NO illustration, NO low-poly, NO stylized 3D rendering. Photographic real-world terrain only.
