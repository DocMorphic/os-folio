# Roadside welcome billboard

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Original PNG is preserved without edits.

Reference direction: the painted wooden Radiator Springs welcome billboard in Cars, adapted to the portfolio's alpine lake and red-and-cream tram. Not the movie logo or Flo's neon sign.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: high-resolution diffuse texture for a physical rooftop roadside billboard in a 3D portfolio.
Primary request: a personalized version of the welcoming, weathered painted wooden Radiator Springs roadside billboard from Cars, with the headline "Dharmay's Tram". Capture the roadside welcome billboard specifically, NOT the Cars red/chrome logo, NOT a neon diner sign.
Composition: wide 3:1 flat rectangular artwork, perfectly straight-on orthographic view, fills the canvas edge to edge. No external background, no mockup, no support posts, no lamps, no perspective, no drop shadow. The 3D frame will be added separately.
Style: beautifully hand-painted mid-century tourism billboard on sun-aged wooden boards; pale turquoise sky, alpine mountains and lake instead of desert mesas. A charming small red-and-cream vintage tram illustration in the lower-right scenery. The typography is the hero: very large warm yellow hand-painted showcard lettering with dark terracotta offset shadow, playful slightly arched arrangement, highly readable at distance. "Dharmay's" takes the main central line, "TRAM" below, with small cream script "Welcome to" above. Small lower-left painted tagline "BUILD COOL SHIT". Spell Dharmay's exactly D H A R M A Y apostrophe S. No other text.
Materials: faint horizontal wood grain and subtle paint wear around edges; not so distressed that it reduces readability. Thin warm-cream painted border inset from edges. Rich but sun-washed colors, affectionate storybook roadside Americana, not a generic flat vector logo.
Lighting: uniform diffuse texture lighting, no baked strong shadows or reflections. No Disney/Pixar logos, no movie characters, no neon, no atomic stars, no boomerang shapes. Output a crisp wide 3:1 landscape image, at least 2048 pixels wide.

## Integration

The image is a diffuse paint texture, mapped directly onto six courses of solid chipped wooden planks, including a split lower board. There is no continuous image plane hiding the gaps. Bare wooden ends and backs, nails, rear rails, braced uprights, bolted roof shoes, and two gooseneck lamps are modeled in `lib/tram-shop-sign.ts`. The full original texture is retained at 2172 × 724 with mipmaps and anisotropic filtering; no bitmap edits were made.
