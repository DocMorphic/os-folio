# Rooftop marquee

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Original artwork; no reference artwork copied.

Research direction: Googie roadside signage, expressive script paired with block capitals, and a distinctive cut-out silhouette.
Reference: https://neonmuseum.org/news/designs-decoded-technical-drawings-from-federal-heath/

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production-ready front-face texture for a dimensional rooftop sign in a 3D tram portfolio. Create ONE original, exceptionally designed mid-century Googie roadside neon marquee, isolated on a genuinely transparent background. Wide landscape canvas 3:1. Text exactly: "Dharmay's" in large elegant, expressive 1950s hand-lettered connected cream script, spelling D-h-a-r-m-a-y apostrophe s; below it "TRAM" in widely spaced bold coral block capitals. ONLY those two words. Make the lettering the hero, beautiful bespoke sign-painter typography, readable at a distance. The sign's backing is a continuous asymmetrical elongated boomerang/pennant silhouette, dark petrol enamel, with a thin cream metal edge and a restrained mint neon perimeter. Small integrated atomic sparkle at the pointed end, connected to the same silhouette. Clear retro-futurist space-age personality matching a cream-and-red vintage tram on a lake. Do NOT make a rectangle, a framed poster, or a rounded rectangular badge. No clip-art tram icon, no logo box, no tiny slogans. Front orthographic view, absolutely no camera perspective or visible side surfaces; crisp precise silhouette and internal artwork. Subtle enamel surface detail, diffuse daylight illumination, no cast shadows outside the sign, no ambient scene, no ground, no supports, no separate disconnected pieces, no watermark. Asset should fill most of the canvas with a narrow transparent margin. No big glowing haze: neon tubes drawn cleanly, the game engine will add lighting. Original design, do not copy a particular existing sign.

## Integration

The PNG retains its original transparency. The application traces its alpha silhouette to build an extruded metal backing, with bolted roof supports. The image is saved in the repo, not referenced from a temporary or machine-specific directory.
