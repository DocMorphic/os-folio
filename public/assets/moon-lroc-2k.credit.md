# Lunar surface texture

NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio — CGI Moon Kit, LROC color map (2025).
Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter / LROC team, NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University.

Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
Original: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg

Used unmodified as the lunar albedo map. The scene deliberately exaggerates the
Moon's angular size; its surface detail comes from lunar imagery.
